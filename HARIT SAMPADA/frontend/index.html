About Harit Sampada

Harit Sampada is a smart crop-planning platform designed to help farmers choose suitable crops based on land, soil, water availability, season, and other agricultural factors.

Developed as a prototype for Smart India Hackathon - SIH26193.

India is an agriculture-rich country, and Harit Sampada aims to empower farmers with data-driven insights for better crop planning and agricultural prosperity. We are one of the largest producers of rice, wheat, sugarcane, and cotton, while still importing some pulses and oilseeds. Keeping this in mind, we have developed this platform to help farmers choose suitable crops based on their land, soil, water availability, season, and other agricultural factors.
